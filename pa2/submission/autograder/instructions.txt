Paste your planner, encoder, decoder files into this folder.
Then to run autograder for

Part1:
To test HPI:
python3 autograder.py --task 1 --algorithm hpi --planner planner.py
To test LP:
python3 autograder.py --task 1 --algorithm lp --planner planner.py

Part 2:
python3 autograder.py --task 2 --planner planner.py --encoder encoder.py --decoder decoder.py

Score Calculation:
Task1 Score = (Public_HPI + Public_LP + Private_HPI + Private_LP)/2

Task2 Score = Public_score + Private_score 

Total Score = Task1 Score + Task2 Score + Report Score
